HINDSIGHT FINANCIAL - NARRATIVE DESIGN DOCUMENT
===============================================
Last updated: 2026-05-29


THESIS CONNECTION
-----------------
This document describes the in-game narrative and world design. The narrative
serves the thesis: adaptive experience-driven financial education through
historical market replay.

The player knowingly relives the 2007-2009 financial crisis through Hindsight
Financial's training program. The story exists to make the adaptive learning
loop feel embodied: trade, experience consequence, receive contextual
instruction, and progress.

The central design rule:
  The player knows the historical era, but not tomorrow.


SETTING
-------
Hindsight Financial is a real institution that trains analysts by reconstructing
historical markets as playable, lived simulations. Its headquarters sits inside
a controlled financial district/campus: part office complex, part city block,
part training environment.

The city is real enough to walk through and work in. Its NPCs are Hindsight
employees, analysts, brokers, media staff, trainees, and commuters attached to
the institution. They know the player is in a training cycle, and they treat
historical crisis as ordinary professional curriculum.

The uncanny tone comes from the institution's calm certainty. Hindsight does
not hide that the player is reliving 2007-2009. Instead, it quietly insists
that knowing the broad ending will not prevent panic, greed, overconfidence, or
bad risk management.

Core institutional line:
  "You know how this ends. That will not help you as much as you think."

Tone:
  - 80% cozy trading life sim / workplace routine.
  - 15% historical pressure and market anxiety.
  - 5% institutional uncanniness.


CORE CHARACTERS
---------------

THE PLAYER
- A new Hindsight trainee entering the 2007-2009 historical replay program.
- Knows the broad historical period and that a crisis is coming.
- Does not receive future dates, future prices, or event spoilers.
- Learns that hindsight is not the same as process.
- Progression through trading skill is narrative progression.

THE PRINCIPAL
- Runs Hindsight Financial.
- Warm, formal, and completely certain.
- Appears rarely, mostly around arc transitions and institutional reviews.
- Never threatens the player. The unsettling quality is confidence, not menace.
- Treats historical catastrophe as an educational instrument.
- Signature idea: knowing the ending does not exempt the player from discipline.

CASEY (MENTOR)
- Friendly, slightly harried, clearly human.
- Genuinely believes in teaching the player.
- Works on Floor 1 / the training floor.
- Serves as tutorial guide, recurring mentor, and performance debrief voice.
- Understands the training program, but not the full institutional agenda.

RECURRING NPC ARCHETYPES
- Analyst: careful, data-driven, explains numbers.
- Broker: sentiment-focused, persuasive, thinks in client psychology.
- Trader: short, direct, practical, emotionally honest about price action.
- Anchor: polished, formal, frames events like financial media.

These archetypes should eventually become named recurring characters rather
than anonymous barks. The thesis benefits when the same market event is
interpreted by distinct voices.


SEQUENCE OF EVENTS
------------------

1. MAIN MENU
   - The player sees the Hindsight Financial title.
   - Visual direction: clean corporate interface with subtle historical-market
     artifacts: ticker tape, newspaper texture, terminal glow, 2007 date stamp.
   - Primary promise:
     "Relive 2007-2009. Trade with hindsight. Learn why it still is not easy."

2. ORIENTATION / ARRIVAL
   - Player enters Hindsight's training district or lobby.
   - The space is real, polished, and slightly too orderly.
   - Screens show the current program:
     "Historical Replay Cycle: 2007-2009 Financial Crisis"
   - The player is not confused about the premise. They are being onboarded.

3. THE PRINCIPAL'S BRIEFING
   - The Principal appears through an office, screen, or staged orientation.
   - Suggested dialogue:
     "Welcome to Hindsight Financial."
     "You are entering the 2007-2009 market cycle."
     "You know the shape of the story. You do not know tomorrow."
     "That distinction is where learning begins."
     "Your task is not to predict history. Your task is to survive it with
      discipline."
   - The player is assigned to Floor 1 / the trainee desk.

4. CASEY'S TUTORIAL
   - The elevator or hallway opens into a warmer training floor.
   - Casey greets the player as a new trainee.
   - Casey dialogue direction:
     "Hey, new cycle? Do not worry. Everyone thinks hindsight will make this
      easy their first week."
     "It helps. It also makes people reckless."
     "Let me show you the rhythm before the market teaches it the hard way."

   Casey teaches three things:

   A) THE DAY CYCLE
      Pre-market is planning. Market hours are observation. Post-market is
      review and response. Then the player rests and advances.

   B) THE KNOWLEDGE LATTICE
      The graph is Hindsight's adaptive curriculum. It unlocks concepts when
      the player's behavior makes them relevant.

   C) FIRST TRADE
      The first trade is not framed as a test of market knowledge. It is the
      first data point in the player's learning profile.

5. DAILY LOOP BEGINS
   - Player trades, reads the newspaper, walks the Hindsight district, hears NPC
     commentary, checks the Knowledge Lattice, and sleeps to advance.
   - The city and UI reinforce that this is a historical replay program.


POST-TUTORIAL NARRATIVE BEATS
-----------------------------

FIRST ADAPTIVE NODE UNLOCK
- A Hindsight memo, desk note, or lattice alert appears.
- It should reference what the player did.
- Casey can soften the moment, but the institution should own the formal
  educational language.

Suggested note tone:
  "Observation: portfolio concentration exceeded safe training thresholds.
   Related concept unlocked: Diversification."

FIRST LOSS
- Casey frames loss as tuition, not failure.
- The Principal's voice can be colder:
  "Loss is only expensive when it teaches nothing."

WEEK 1 COMPLETE
- Casey reflects on the player's behavior, not only their return.
- The player sees other trainees entering different historical cycles.
- This establishes Hindsight as an institution, not a dream.

ARC TRANSITION: BULL MARKET TO THE UNRAVELING
- The city should become subtly less confident.
- NPC barks shift from optimism to uncertainty.
- The Principal reminds the player that knowing "a crisis is coming" is not the
  same as knowing what to do today.

ARC TRANSITION: THE UNRAVELING TO MELTDOWN
- The city becomes quieter, colder, more operational.
- Media NPCs and analyst NPCs become more urgent.
- Grading should emphasize survival, liquidity, and process.

ENDGAME DIRECTION
- The player completes the 2007-2009 cycle.
- Hindsight evaluates more than returns: discipline, diversification, cash
  management, loss control, and completed lessons.
- The final reflection should ask what hindsight actually gave the player.
- Leave the Principal's broader agenda ambiguous, but not random.


DESIGN PRINCIPLES
-----------------

1. HINDSIGHT IS THE HOOK AND THE TRAP
   The player knows the broad history. The game teaches that broad history does
   not replace disciplined process.

2. LEARNING IS THE PLOT
   The player's progression through trading concepts is narrative progression.
   The Knowledge Lattice is not a side menu. It is the institution's curriculum
   reacting to the player's behavior.

3. THE CITY IS AN INFORMATION ENVIRONMENT
   NPCs, newspapers, HUDs, terminals, and arc ambience should all interpret the
   same market from different angles. The player is surrounded by partial
   information, sentiment, pressure, and hindsight bias.

4. KEEP THE COZY WORKPLACE RHYTHM
   The player needs enough comfort to experiment. The day cycle, room, Casey,
   and routine make the project approachable.

5. LET THE UNCANNY STAY QUIET
   The institution should feel strange because it is too calm about crisis, not
   because it is openly hostile. No jump scares. No heavy surrealism unless it
   directly supports the training premise.

6. SHOW, DON'T LECTURE
   Narrative beats should arise from trades, node completions, arc transitions,
   days elapsed, and portfolio outcomes. Avoid long forced cutscenes after the
   initial orientation.

7. CASEY IS REAL
   Casey genuinely cares. If Hindsight has a hidden agenda, Casey is not simply
   a fake guide. Her sincerity protects the game's warmth.

DESIGNED, POST-DEMO:
- Dual-path triggers: negative/positive/time-based paths to the same node.
  Addresses failure-bias in current triggers. A careful player who diversifies
  from day one should still unlock the diversification node via a positive
  trigger ("You've maintained a balanced portfolio for 10 days").
- Weekend review: "Let's review what happened this week." Server advances N
  days with compressed summary. Narratively framed as Hindsight controlling
  replay speed. Casey introduces: "We can fast-forward through the quiet
  weeks if you want." Addresses pacing (148 days × 5 min = 12+ hours per
  arc without this).

KNOWLEDGE LATTICE - FIRST 3 NODES
---------------------------------

Node 1: "What is a Company"
  - Casey explains businesses, ownership, and why companies exist.
  - Unlocked at game start.

Node 2: "What is a Stock"
  - Casey explains shares, ownership stakes, and why prices move.
  - Unlocked after completing Node 1.

Node 3: "Market Buy / Sell"
  - Casey walks player through their first actual trade.
  - Completing this node ends the guided tutorial and begins free play.

The first three nodes should feel like Casey's warm training. Later adaptive
nodes can feel more institutional and analytical.


DIALOGUE STYLE GUIDE
--------------------

CASEY
- Casual, warm, uses contractions.
- Honest about mistakes and pressure.
- Never condescending.
- Example:
  "Yeah, hindsight helps. It also makes people overconfident. Ask me how I
   learned that."

THE PRINCIPAL
- Formal but not stiff.
- Short declarative sentences.
- Does not ask questions; states conclusions.
- Unnerving because of calm certainty.
- Example:
  "You know the ending. Good. Now prove you understand the path."

PLAYER INTERNAL VOICE
- Less amnesia, more reflective uncertainty.
- The player understands the premise but questions their own decisions.
- Example early:
  "I know what happens in 2008. Why does this still feel like guessing?"
- Example later:
  "Maybe knowing the crash is coming was the easy part."

TYPED NOTES / HINDSIGHT MEMOS
- Clinical, precise, educational, lightly institutional.
- No warmth. Pure analysis.
- Example:
  "Volatility measures variation in price over time. Your position exceeded the
   daily movement threshold. Related risk concept unlocked."

NPC ARCHETYPES
- Analyst: measured, numerical, explanatory.
- Broker: sentiment and client psychology.
- Trader: short, emotional, price-action focused.
- Anchor: broadcast-style market framing.
