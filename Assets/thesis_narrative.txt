HINDSIGHT FINANCIAL - THESIS NARRATIVE & FRAMING
================================================
Created: 2026-05-26
Updated: 2026-05-29


UNIFIED THESIS
--------------
Static financial education fails because students memorize definitions without
building intuition. Trading simulators improve on textbooks but remain
fundamentally static: same lessons, same feedback, same content regardless of
what the student actually does.

Hindsight Financial bridges this gap with an adaptive game-based learning loop
centered on a reactive knowledge graph and reinforced by LLM-generated context.
The player knowingly relives the 2007-2009 financial crisis, but with one key
constraint: they know the era, not tomorrow. This preserves historical
hindsight as a framing device without turning the game into trivia arbitrage.

The thesis contribution is one integrated system with three mutually dependent
parts:

  1. GAME LOOP as the EXPERIENCE GENERATOR
     The day cycle, trading terminal, arc system, city hub, and portfolio
     consequences create lived financial experiences. A student who trades
     through the 2008 crisis remembers what volatility, overconfidence, and
     panic feel like, not just what those terms mean.

  2. REACTIVE KNOWLEDGE GRAPH as the PEDAGOGICAL MECHANISM
     The graph detects meaningful player behavior and unlocks instruction at
     the moment it is most relevant. It is the core thesis mechanism: learning
     is delivered through the player's own decisions.

  3. LLM-GENERATED CONTENT as the CONTEXTUALIZATION LAYER
     Newspapers, NPC commentary, and adaptive lesson language make the world
     respond to the player's current market date, portfolio, and recent trades.
     The LLM layer turns static historical data into situated feedback.

These are not three separate contributions. They are three layers of one loop:
the game creates experiences, the server records them, the knowledge graph
classifies them pedagogically, and LLM content makes them legible inside the
world.

THE CENTRAL CONTRIBUTION
------------------------
The reactive knowledge graph is the thesis's crown jewel. It is the mechanism
by which gameplay, adaptivity, and instruction converge.

  - Traditional approach: Student reads Chapter 5 on risk management after
    finishing Chapter 4. Linear. Context-free. Forgettable.

  - Hindsight approach: Student over-concentrates their portfolio in one stock.
    The knowledge graph detects this via the "portfolio_concentration" trigger.
    A node on diversification unlocks. The content explains exactly what the
    player did and why it matters. The lesson arrives at the moment of maximum
    relevance.

This is constructivist learning operationalized through game mechanics: the
student constructs understanding from their own actions, and the system ensures
the right scaffolding appears when that action has made the concept meaningful.

The behavioral triggers currently implemented include:
  - trade_count               (trading activity threshold)
  - held_stock_daily_change   (experiencing price movement)
  - portfolio_concentration   (over-allocation risk)
  - first_profitable_sell     (experiencing profit)
  - first_losing_sell         (experiencing loss)
  - same_ticker_multiple_buys (averaging down/up behavior)
  - unrealized_gain_pct       (paper gains awareness)
  - unrealized_loss_pct       (deep unrealized loss)
  - bought_after_decline      (buy-the-dip behavior)
  - low_cash_ratio            (cash management)
  - market_wide_decline       (systemic risk exposure)
  - traded_both_phases        (market timing awareness)
  - held_overnight_gap        (overnight gap risk)

Each trigger maps to a real pedagogical moment. This is not gamification for
engagement alone. It is gameplay as curriculum delivery.

DUAL-PATH TRIGGERS (DESIGNED, NOT YET IMPLEMENTED)
The current triggers assume failure as the entry point for learning. But what
if the player does well? What if they diversify from day one? The system should
recognize both failure and success as pedagogical moments.

Each adaptive node would support three trigger paths:
  - NEGATIVE: Current failure-based triggers (e.g., concentration > 80%)
  - POSITIVE: Success-based triggers (e.g., maintained diversification across
    4+ stocks for N days, kept cash reserves above 20% for N days)
  - TIME-BASED: Fallback unlock after N trading days to ensure curriculum
    completeness regardless of player behavior

Same node, same concept, different trigger_explanation and personalized
content. The negative path says "You concentrated 82% in NVDA — here's why
diversification matters." The positive path says "You've maintained a balanced
portfolio across 4 sectors — here's the principle behind what you're already
doing." The time-based path ensures no concept is permanently locked behind
a behavior the player may never exhibit.

This addresses a pedagogical gap: the system should teach through both
corrective feedback (you made a mistake, here's why) and reinforcement
(you're doing well, here's why that works). The architecture supports this
without changes to PlayerContextService — it already provides the raw data
that all three paths would evaluate against.


UNIFIED PLAYER CONTEXT (IMPLEMENTED 2026-05-31)
The three thesis layers (game loop, knowledge graph, LLM content) are connected
through PlayerContextService — a unified snapshot of the player's trading
behavior, portfolio state, and learning progress. This is the connective tissue
that makes "one loop" real rather than aspirational.

Each LLM consumer draws from the same canonical profile:
  - NPC dialogue fact sheets include PLAYER KNOWLEDGE section
  - DynamicNodeContentService generates personalized node explanations
    referencing the player's actual trades and portfolio
  - Newspaper player sidebar cross-references holdings with market movers

The service assembles raw data blocks (market snapshot, portfolio, trades,
knowledge state, arc info). Each consumer selects and formats what it needs.
The service does not know about NPC personalities or newspaper editorial
tone — it provides the data layer.


WHY HISTORICAL HINDSIGHT WORKS
------------------------------
The player knows they are entering 2007-2009. That knowledge is part of the
fiction and the learning design. The point is not to test whether the player
remembers exact dates; the point is to show that broad historical knowledge is
not the same as disciplined decision-making.

The player may know that a crisis is coming, but they still face uncertainty:
  - Which stocks are vulnerable today?
  - How much cash should be kept in reserve?
  - When is a dip an opportunity versus a trap?
  - How long can the player stay rational while prices move against them?
  - Does knowing the ending prevent emotional mistakes?

This turns hindsight into the game's central tension. The player has the
advantage of era-level knowledge, but the game teaches that investing still
requires process, risk management, and self-control.


HOW THE LLM LAYER REINFORCES THIS
---------------------------------
The LLM-generated content serves three functions:

  1. NEWSPAPER ("The Market Tribune")
     - Generated per game-day using Claude API.
     - Prompt context includes arc tone, real historical events near the date,
       market movers, and available player context.
     - Purpose: present-tense historical immersion. The player reads the day
       as it would have felt then, not as a retrospective summary.

  2. NPC DIALOGUE
     - Generated or selected based on current phase, recent trades, portfolio
       state, market conditions, and NPC personality type.
     - Analyst, broker, trader, and anchor archetypes provide conflicting
       interpretations of the same market.
     - Purpose: model the real information environment, where investors must
       synthesize partial, biased, and emotionally charged perspectives.

  3. ADAPTIVE KNOWLEDGE CONTENT
     - When a behavioral trigger fires, the unlocked node can explain the
       player's action in context.
     - Purpose: personalized instruction. The system does not merely say
       "diversification matters"; it can connect that idea to the player's
       concentrated position and its consequences.


NARRATIVE PREMISE
-----------------
Hindsight Financial is a real institution built around historical market
replay. It trains new analysts by placing them inside reconstructed financial
periods and evaluating how they act under uncertainty.

The player knows they are entering a 2007-2009 training cycle. Hindsight does
not hide the historical era. Instead, it insists that knowing the broad ending
will not save the player from bad process.

Core institutional line:
  "You know how this ends. That will not help you as much as you think."

The city is a real Hindsight district/campus: offices, terminals, market media,
employees, trainees, and public spaces all orbit the institution's training
mission. The uncanny quality comes from how calmly the institution treats
historical crisis as curriculum.


NARRATIVE FLOW FOR DEMO
-----------------------
The demo should walk through one complete game day to show the integrated
adaptive learning loop:

  1. PRE-MARKET PHASE
     - Player is in the Room or Hindsight training floor.
     - Show the current arc/date and establish that this is a historical
       replay of 2007-2009.
     - Read the newspaper for today's date.
     - Queue a buy order and show it in the pending queue.
     - Talking point: "The player knows the era, but not the day's outcome.
       The newspaper reconstructs the market's present-tense information
       environment."

  2. DAY PHASE
     - Transition to City. Timer counts down.
     - NPCs move through the Hindsight district.
     - Walk near an NPC and show contextual dialogue about market conditions.
     - Visit the knowledge graph terminal.
     - Talking point: "The city is not just scenery. It is an information
       environment around the player's trading behavior."

  3. POST-MARKET PHASE
     - Timer expires or player returns to Room.
     - Daily summary overlay shows trades, P&L, movers, and portfolio state.
     - Player can queue and confirm orders at the close price.
     - A knowledge graph trigger fires.
     - Open the graph and show the newly unlocked node.
     - Talking point: "The system detected what the player experienced and
       surfaced the relevant educational content at that moment."

  4. ARC CONTEXT
     - Show current arc, days remaining, return, and projected grade.
     - Explain that arc performance affects starting capital in later arcs.
     - Talking point: "The arc system turns historical market periods into
       staged learning conditions: confidence, uncertainty, panic, survival."

PAPER STRUCTURE
---------------
ABSTRACT
- Problem: static financial education lacks experiential engagement.
- Approach: historical trading sim with reactive knowledge graph and LLM
  contextualization.
- Contribution: an adaptive game-based learning loop that delivers instruction
  based on player behavior.

1. INTRODUCTION
- Financial literacy gap.
- Limits of textbooks and static trading simulators.
- Hindsight Financial premise.
- Contribution summary.

2. RELATED WORK
- Game-based learning and situated cognition.
- Adaptive learning systems and knowledge tracing.
- LLMs in education.
- Financial literacy tools and trading simulators.
- Knowledge graphs in pedagogy.

3. SYSTEM DESIGN
- Design philosophy: learning is produced by play.
- Historical hindsight as a design constraint.
- Three-part contribution: game loop, reactive graph, LLM context.
- Arc system and day cycle.
- City as information environment.

4. IMPLEMENTATION
- Unity client, ASP.NET Core API, SQLite, Claude API.
- Server-authoritative state.
- Trading engine and order pipeline.
- Knowledge graph trigger evaluation.
- PlayerContextService: unified player profiling as the connective tissue
  between game loop, knowledge graph, and LLM content generation.
- DynamicNodeContentService: personalized knowledge explanations generated
  on trigger, referencing the player's actual portfolio and trades.
- LLM generation, caching, and fallback strategies.

5. DISCUSSION
- Adaptive triggers versus linear curriculum.
- Failure-biased vs dual-path triggers: current system assumes failure as
  entry point for learning. Designed extension supports positive triggers
  (recognizing good behavior) and time-based fallbacks (curriculum
  completeness). Same architecture, different trigger descriptions.
- Hindsight as advantage and trap.
- LLM reliability and factual grounding.
- Server authority and academic integrity.
- Pacing tension: historical fidelity vs player time. 148 trading days
  at 5 minutes each exceeds reasonable session length. Weekend review
  (compressed weekly summaries) and adaptive day pacing (interestingness-
  driven compression) as designed solutions.
- AI-assisted development methodology.

6. LIMITATIONS AND FUTURE WORK
- No formal user study yet.
- Single-player prototype.
- Limited historical period and limited authored narrative.
- Triggers are currently failure-biased; dual-path (positive/negative/time)
  triggers designed but not implemented.
- No skip-week or adaptive pacing; each day requires full play-through.
- Ticker symbols use modern names (GOOGL not GOOG); prices are correct but
  display names break period immersion. Fixable via a small override table.
- Future classroom dashboard, multi-student deployment, learning outcome
  measurement, procedural historical arcs.

7. CONCLUSION
- Hindsight Financial demonstrates how a trading game can turn player behavior
  into teachable moments through a reactive knowledge graph and contextual LLM
  feedback.


WHY THIS MATTERS
----------------
The argument is not simply "games are better than textbooks." The stronger
argument is: "What happens when a game creates financial experiences, a
knowledge graph identifies their pedagogical meaning, and generative AI
contextualizes them inside a responsive world?"

Hindsight Financial sits at that intersection. The game creates the experience.
The knowledge graph decides why it matters. The LLM helps the experience teach.
